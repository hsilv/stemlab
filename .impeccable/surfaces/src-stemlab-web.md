# Deck

Mode: operate

The person preparing tracks on this machine loads a file, marks a section, chooses an output, and separates it. Every current control stays. The short introduction stays, in deck language. The dim room is the reference light. A light show is out.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a deck and a crate, and it refuses the headline-and-cards studio. The job walks one plate at a time.

OWN-WORLD: Matte black deck (#0c0c0e), aluminum faceplate (#d5d3ce), paper type (#f2f0ea), and amber (#e0a15a) for the active step, the cue, and Separate. Stem channels carry their own color: vocals amber, drums #d4654a, bass #3f8f78, other #8b87b0. Square edges, hairline rules, Barlow for labels and controls. The waveform is ink.

STORY: Load a track, choose the colored stems, mark every stretch where the effect should play, then watch the real stage move the bar. The crate holds the jobs. The file stays on this machine.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The crate is a dense list on the left. The deck fills the rest, under "Load a track.": a step rail (Load, Stems, Ranges, Process) and one page. Load is the drop plate. Stems is four colored channel keys plus the output modes and quality. Ranges is the waveform, with every marked stretch painted on the ink. Process is the stage name and a bar that moves only when the stage does.

FORM: The crate, first on the grounded list and the chosen pick. Seed key f79f547a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
